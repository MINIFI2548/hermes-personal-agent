# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 16 October 2026_

**Hermes Personal Agent** ("the app") is a private tool used only by its owner, Sarayuth Sainiyom, to manage his own Google account data.

## Data accessed
With the owner's explicit consent via Google OAuth, the app can read and modify the owner's Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Contacts.

## How data is used
Data is used only to perform tasks requested by the owner (e.g. creating calendar events, searching email, editing documents).

## Storage and sharing
- OAuth tokens and any fetched data are stored locally on the owner's own devices.
- Data is not sold, shared with, or disclosed to any third party, and is not used for advertising.
- The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Revoking access
The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions and can delete locally stored tokens.
